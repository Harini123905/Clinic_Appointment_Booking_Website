# Kaveri Family Clinic Knowledge Base

## About the clinic
Kaveri Family Clinic is a friendly neighbourhood clinic offering general medicine, child health, skin care, bone and joint care, and women's health under one roof. Patients can book online at any time. Walk-in patients are welcome, but booked patients are seen first.

## Opening hours
The clinic is open Monday to Saturday, from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The doctors take a lunch break from 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM, so no appointments are available at that time. The clinic is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Appointment length and booking
Every appointment is 30 minutes long. Appointments can be booked up to 30 days ahead through the website. Each doctor works on set days of the week, so only those days can be chosen for that doctor. After booking, the patient receives a reference number that starts with CLN.

## Cancelling or changing an appointment
To cancel or change an appointment, please call reception at least 4 hours before the appointment time and quote the booking reference. This lets another patient use the slot.

## Fees and cost of a consultation
A standard consultation costs 600 rupees. A specialist consultation costs 900 rupees. A follow-up visit within 7 days of the first visit costs 300 rupees. Payment can be made by cash, card or UPI at the reception desk.

## What to bring
Please bring a photo ID, any previous prescriptions or test reports, and a list of medicines you are currently taking. For children, please bring the vaccination card.

## Which doctor should I see?
This section gives general guidance only. It is not medical advice and it is not a diagnosis. If unsure, start with the General Physician.

### Fever, cold, cough, sore throat, body ache, tiredness, stomach upset
speciality: General Physician
For common illnesses such as fever, cold, cough, sore throat, body ache, tiredness, stomach upset, blood pressure or sugar check-ups, the General Physician is the best first visit.

### Children's health, baby care, child fever, vaccinations, growth
speciality: Paediatrician
For babies, children and teenagers, including vaccinations, slow growth, child fever, or common childhood illness, see the Paediatrician.

### Skin rash, itching, acne, pimples, eczema, hair fall, dandruff, nail problems
speciality: Dermatologist
For skin, hair and nail concerns such as rashes, itching, acne, pimples, eczema, hair fall or dandruff, see the Dermatologist.

### Joint pain, back pain, neck pain, knee pain, sports injury, sprain, bone health
speciality: Orthopaedic Specialist
For joint pain, back pain, neck pain, knee pain, sprains, sports injuries or bone health concerns, see the Orthopaedic Specialist.

### Women's health, periods, irregular periods, pregnancy care, check-up
speciality: Gynaecologist
For women's health check-ups, period problems, irregular periods or pregnancy care, see the Gynaecologist.

## Emergencies
The clinic does not handle emergencies. For severe chest pain, trouble breathing, heavy bleeding, fainting, stroke signs or any life-threatening problem, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest hospital emergency department immediately.
